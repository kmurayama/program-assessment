## Title:
Developing Leadership Skills through Assessment (or Using Assessment to Improve Leadership Skills, or The Role of Assessment in Developing Leaders for a Sustainable World) 

## Abstract (40 words):
Leadership skills for a sustainable world are developed through the design, implementation, and analysis of a robust assessment of student learning outcomes program.   The unique integration of internal and external assessment data allows the institution to track the development of leadership skills and drive curriculum improvements to achieve continuous improvement. 

## Description (200 words):  
Development of leadership skills consists of multilayered learning stages. Learners need to establish solid content knowledge, apply acquired concepts into practice, and evaluate effectiveness and relevance to the problem at hand. As educators, then, we want to improve our leadership curriculum through multi-dimensional assessment. Here is what out department did to meet this challenge. First, we established learning objectives for the core curriculum and programs. Then, each of those learning objectives are evaluated through internal and external assessment tools. The aim of our design is to have the internal and external assessment tools be complementary of each other: external tools to test lower-level learning, and internal tools to measure higher-level learning. Moreover, some indirect measures obtained through exit surveys provide more subjective assessments of students' learning to fill the gaps between objective assessments and learning objectives defined above. Lastly, the findings from those data/observations are brought back to the faculty at periodical, strategic meetings. Combining these, we identify opportunities for improvements and enhancements, develop strategies to implement improvements to our curriculum, and maintain a constant feedback loop of implementation, assessment, and reflection. Our contributions to the field of leadership education and assessment are two-fold: first, our case highlights a novel method for integrating distinctive assessment processes to yield multidimensional assessment of learning, which is essential in leadership skill development; second, the implementation of our method demonstrates a holistic approach to assessment and curriculum development, representing a well-structured yet flexible alternative to  approaches that yield an abundance of assessment data but which lack structure and integration.  

 
## Learning Objective (230 words):
After this presentation, attendees will be able to employ an advanced assessment of student learning outcomes methodology to develop leadership skills at their institution. 

 
## Any other reasons this should be accepted (100 words): 
The researchers believe their method of integrating internal and external assessment data, the use of D2L rubrics and automated data retrieval, and the use R to perform data analysis could be broadly applied at other institutions to improve assessment regimes beyond the scope of developing leadership skills.
